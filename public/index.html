    Akwaaba · Welcome home

    A campus fellowship.
    A family in Christ.

    We are the National Union of Baptist Students at the University of Ghana, Legon.

    Our local fellowship is part of NUBS Ghana, a Christian student movement that brings Baptist and non-Baptist students together for fellowship, discipleship, leadership development and Christian service.

    At the heart of our shared identity is a commitment to grow in Christ and put faith into action, on campus and beyond.

    Our mission

    Grow in faith.
    Go out in service.

    NUBS Ghana's mission brings together evangelism, discipleship, student leadership and care for students' spiritual, academic and socio-economic needs.

    Our vision

    Christ's mission.
    Every campus.
